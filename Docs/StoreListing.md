## Description

Every tower is a puzzle. Every victory makes you stronger.

Choose an enemy with less power than your hero, swipe toward its room, and add its strength to yours. Plan your route through the tower: a foe you cannot defeat now may become your next victory after a few clever choices.

- Explore 24 keeps across four chapters.
- Watch defeated rooms fall and the surviving tower settle into place.
- Earn gold and gems for first clears and collect a daily gift.
- Unlock four heroes, three shields and nine weapons with earned-currency chests.
- Rotate your hero, preview equipment and build your own look.
- Pause whenever you need, with music, sound, haptics, reduced-motion and battery-saving options.
- Play offline with progress saved on your device.

No accounts, ads or in-app purchases. Your tactics decide the battle; your equipment decides your style.

## Reviewer notes

The first Adventure action shows the rules. Blue numbers are hero power; red numbers are enemy power. Swipe right toward a weaker enemy. The first keep starts at 10 power with enemy values 2, 16 and 8 in its first tower: defeat 2, then 8, then 16. Every stage has a validated winning route.

Gold/gems are local, non-purchasable game rewards. Chest odds appear on the Shop screen. Cosmetics are visual and do not alter puzzle power. There is no login, payment, subscription, chat or network dependency to review. Armor contains shields; the green and stone cloaks are native parts of their respective hero models.
